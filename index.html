Watching o3 guess a photo's location is surreal, dystopian and entertaining

文章摘要

Simon Willison分享了使用OpenAI的o3模型根据照片猜测拍摄地点的体验。该模型通过分析照片特征（如房屋样式、植被、车牌等），结合其视觉处理能力，逐步缩小地点。尽管初期出现无法“看见”图片的错误，最终模型成功推测出照片拍摄于加州中部海岸，虽然具体地点有误，但其“次选猜测”准确无误。整个过程类似科幻情节，通过运行Python代码进行图像处理和分析，展现了强大的图像识别和推理能力。实验显示模型并未依赖照片的EXIF数据作弊。尽管结果非完全精准，但技术令人印象深刻。

Barlow's Principles of Adult Behaviour

文章摘要

John Perry Barlow's "Principles of Adult Behavior" is a list of 25 guidelines he created on his 30th birthday to foster personal growth and responsibility. The principles emphasize patience, accountability, tolerance, humility, self-reflection, and respect for others. Barlow encourages embracing ambiguity, laughing at oneself, admitting mistakes, and seeking purpose over happiness. He stresses honesty, avoiding harm to others, and living a life that is memorable and loving towards oneself. Although not expecting perfection, Barlow asks friends to hold him accountable to these standards.
