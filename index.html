文章摘要

Preston Thorpe, a prison inmate, shares his journey of becoming a software engineer for Turso while incarcerated. After making poor life choices related to drugs in his twenties, Preston enrolled in a prison college program that provided limited computer access, reigniting his passion for programming. He dedicated 15+ hours daily to coding and open-source projects, eventually joining a remote work program and leading the development team at Unlocked Labs. Preston later contributed to Turso's Project Limbo, which involved rewriting SQLite. His hard work and dedication caught the attention of Turso's founders, leading to a full-time position. Preston's story serves as an inspiration, showcasing how determination and discipline can lead to unexpected opportunities, even from behind bars.

作者: rorosen | 评分: 104 | 评论数: 9 | 发布时间: 2025-06-15 20:49
文章摘要

**Zeekstd** 是一个 Rust 实现的 Zstandard 可检索格式（Zstandard Seekable Format）。该格式将压缩数据分割成一系列独立的“帧”，每帧单独压缩，允许从档案中间直接解压特定部分，而无需解压整个文件。Zeekstd 在兼容初始版本的基础上，对该格式进行了增强。它支持自动分帧压缩以及按需解压特定帧。项目还包含一个命令行工具，便于使用该库。该项目使用 BSD-2-Clause 许可证。
