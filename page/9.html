Honesty Boxes in Scotland (2024)

作者: NaOH | 评分: 28 | 评论数: 16 | 发布时间: 2025-08-07 14:11
文章摘要

Honesty boxes in Scotland are a charming rural tradition reflecting trust and community spirit. These unstaffed stands, found across the countryside, offer local products like fresh eggs, vegetables, baked goods, crafts, and even seafood. Notable examples include the Ardnamurchan Community Garden Shop, which provides seasonal produce, and Comrie Golf Club, where golfers pay via an honesty box outside staffed hours. Honesty camping at Valtos Beach allows for a donation-based camping experience, while Inverlussa Mussels offers sustainably farmed mussels directly from the shore. Shetland's cake fridges feature homemade treats, relying on customer honesty for payment. This practice highlights Scotland's community values and is a unique aspect of the country's culture.

Show HN: Aura – Like robots.txt, but for AI actions

作者: OsmanDKitay | 评分: 33 | 评论数: 10 | 发布时间: 2025-08-06 13:37
文章摘要

AURA（Agent-Usable Resource Assertion）是一个开放协议，旨在使网站变得机器可读，替代脆弱的屏幕抓取和DOM操作。它通过一个声明式的`aura.json` manifest文件，让网站以安全、高效和标准化的API形式向AI代理暴露其功能。核心概念包括Manifest文件（位于`/.well-known/aura.json`）定义网站能力，Capability描述具体操作（如`create_post`），以及动态HTTP头`AURA-State`提供上下文信息。该协议提供了NPM包和参考服务器实现，帮助网站实现AURA兼容，并展示了如何通过AI代理执行网站功能。这将改变AI与网站交互的方式，从猜测性操作转变为声明式交互。
