Dropbox CEO Drew Houston has voiced strong support for remote work, criticizing companies that enforce return-to-office (RTO) mandates. Despite some studies suggesting in-office or hybrid models promote work-life balance, many workers prefer remote work, with some even willing to take pay cuts for flexibility. However, companies like Amazon and EA are pushing for RTO mandates, reflecting ongoing controversy in the tech industry.
