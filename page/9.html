Apache ECharts

作者: tomtomistaken | 评分: 1597 | 评论数: 89 | 发布时间: 2025-04-08 17:23
文章摘要

Apache ECharts是一款开源的JavaScript可视化库，提供超过20种图表类型和多种组件，支持任意组合使用。它具有强大的渲染引擎，可在Canvas和SVG之间轻松切换，支持渐进渲染和流加载，能够实时渲染大量数据。ECharts还提供专业的数据分析功能，通过数据集管理支持过滤、聚类和回归等数据转换。其默认设计遵循可视化原则，支持响应式设计，配置灵活易于定制。ECharts拥有活跃的开源社区，提供无障碍功能，自动生成图表描述和图案帮助残障用户理解内容。用户在研发项目、产品、研究论文等中使用ECharts时，可引用2018年发表的论文《Visual Informatics》。

The overlooked psychedelic that may help treat traumatic brain injury

作者: PaulHoule | 评分: 5 | 评论数: 1 | 发布时间: 2025-04-11 02:34
文章摘要

Ibogaine, a psychedelic derived from an African shrub, shows promise in treating traumatic brain injury (TBI) and PTSD, according to 2024 Stanford research. In a study, military veterans treated with ibogaine in Mexico experienced significant improvements in mood, anxiety, and cognitive function. Despite these findings, ibogaine remains a Schedule I substance in the U.S. due to safety concerns, particularly related to heart health. Traditionally used in Central Africa for rituals, ibogaine gained attention in the West for its anti-addiction properties in the 1960s, but clinical trials are limited. The recent study on veterans revealed an 80%+ reduction in PTSD, depression, and anxiety symptoms post-treatment, highlighting its potential for TBI therapy, a challenging condition to treat. However, safety issues and past deaths at retreats underscore the need for further research and regulation.
