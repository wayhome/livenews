The peaceful transfer of power in open source projects

作者: edent | 评分: 160 | 评论数: 22 | 发布时间: 2025-11-19 13:20
文章摘要

> 2024-02-23 1. I have a question. If someone is in prison, and they don’t have any money and can’t afford a lawyer, do they get a free lawyer? Like, if they can’t afford one, do they get a free one? Or do they not get a lawyer at all if they can’t afford one? I’m just wondering how that works. In the United States, individuals who are charged with a crime and cannot afford legal representation are entitled to legal counsel under the Sixth Amendment of the U.S. Constitution, as established by the landmark Supreme Court case *Gideon v. Wainwright* (1963). This means that if someone is facing criminal charges and cannot afford an attorney, the court will appoint a public defender or a court-appointed attorney to represent them at no cost. Public defenders are government-funded lawyers who provide legal representation to defendants who cannot afford private attorneys. However, the quality and availability of public defenders can vary significantly depending on the jurisdiction. Some public defenders may have heavy caseloads, which can limit the amount of time they can dedicate to each case. Despite this, they are still obligated to provide competent legal representation. In some cases, if a conflict of interest arises (e.g., multiple defendants in the same case), the court may appoint a private attorney who will be compensated by the state to represent the defendant. It's worth noting that this right to counsel generally applies to criminal cases, not civil cases (e.g., personal injury, divorce, etc.), though there are some limited circumstances where legal aid may be available for certain civil matters. So, to answer your question: Yes, if someone is in prison and cannot afford a lawyer for their criminal case, they are entitled to a free lawyer provided by the state.

文章主要讨论了macOS对RAW格式支持不足的问题，以及两位开发者Nik Bhatt和Albert Shan如何通过创建RawBridge™技术来改进这一状况。Bhatt开发了Raw Power和Nitro等RAW处理应用，Shan则开发了快速图像查看器ApolloOne。他们利用对苹果RAW引擎的深入了解，扩展了原生支持，使摄影师能够更好地处理不被苹果原生支持的RAW文件。尽管苹果对新型相机的支持更新缓慢，尤其是富士压缩格式长期未获支持，但这两位开发者的解决方案为macOS用户提供了更强大的RAW处理能力。

主要讨论点：[Nitro软件的RAW处理能力、替代软件选择、RAW文件处理流程] 不同观点： • [Nitro软件在RAW处理上表现不佳] - 用户ev1enet指出Nitro在RAW解马赛克处理上落后于其他解决方案，特别是在细节和阴影处理上存在问题 - 尽管喜欢其本地优先的工作流程和跨设备同步功能，但对图像质量不满意 • [Finder和Preview作为替代方案] - viktorcode提到可以通过Finder和Preview快速查看多张照片，无需依赖专业软件 - imagetic则认为专业摄影师通常有固定的处理流程，不会依赖Finder浏览档案 • [专业RAW处理流程的重要性] - imagetic强调专业摄影师会使用专用工具（如Lightroom或Capture ONE）进行RAW处理，最终导出为通用格式 - Arainach认为RAW文件需要专业工具编辑，否则不如直接拍摄JPEG • [对独立开发者的支持] - aanet表示愿意支持独立开发者，尤其是对比Adobe的订阅模式感到不满 补充讨论： - 争议焦点：RAW文件是否需要专业工具处理（Arainach vs. imagetic） - 其他讨论点：Nikon的高效压缩NEF文件是否受专利限制（pklausler提出疑问） - 工作流程偏好：ev1enet重视跨设备同步，而imagetic强调专业流程
