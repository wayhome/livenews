Why Most Vaccines Don't Contain Mercury Anymore

作者: Brajeshwar | 评分: 3 | 评论数: 1 | 发布时间: 2025-06-26 14:17
文章摘要

Thimerosal, a mercury-containing compound used as a preservative in vaccines since the 1930s, has largely been phased out from childhood vaccines. It is composed of ethylmercury, which is different from the more toxic methylmercury found in fish. Ethylmercury is quickly cleared from the body within three to seven days, making it much less likely to cause harm. Methylmercury, on the other hand, accumulates in the body and can cause neurological issues. Despite the safety of thimerosal, it was removed from childhood vaccines as a precautionary measure in the late 1990s during broader efforts to reduce mercury exposure. However, it is still used in some adult vaccines because it effectively prevents contamination during manufacturing. Studies have shown thimerosal does not cause neurodevelopmental disorders, and its use in adult vaccines continues to be considered safe.
