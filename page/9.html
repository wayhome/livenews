作者: 082349872349872 | 评分: 155 | 评论数: 9 | 发布时间: 2025-04-03 08:34
文章摘要

这篇文章简要介绍了锻造作为一种爱好，重点描述了锻造中使用的材料和工具，特别是 mild steel（低碳钢）的特性以及如何使用铁钳。低碳钢在高温下变得柔软易塑，可以通过锤击成型，即使出错也可以重新加热修正。铁钳是锻造中用于夹持高温金属的重要工具，如果找不到合适的铁钳，可以自己动手锻造一对定制铁钳，方法是将两根棍状材料用铆钉连接，初步成型后进一步调整。锻造过程中经常需要临时制作工具来辅助完成最终目标，这也类似于编程中的自举问题。锻造的缺点是高温操作容易导致轻微烧伤，但通过实践可以学会如何避免严重伤害。

SeedLM: Compressing LLM Weights into Seeds of Pseudo-Random Generators

作者: pizza | 评分: 166 | 评论数: 13 | 发布时间: 2025-04-06 08:53
文章摘要

**SeedLM: Compressing LLM Weights into Seeds of Pseudo-Random Generators** *Authors: Rasoul Shafipour et al.* Published in ICLR 2025 SeedLM introduces a novel post-training compression method for Large Language Models (LLMs) that uses seeds of pseudo-random generators to encode and compress model weights. For each weight block, a seed is found and used during inference to generate a random matrix via a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR). This matrix, combined with compressed coefficients, reconstructs the weight block. SeedLM reduces memory access by trading compute for fewer memory operations, achieving speed-ups in memory-bound tasks. Unlike other methods, SeedLM is data-free and generalizes well across tasks. Experiments with Llama3 70B show that SeedLM retains zero-shot accuracy at 4- and 3-bit compression, comparable to or better than state-of-the-art methods, and FPGA tests indicate a 4x speed-up over FP16 baselines as model size increases.
