文章摘要

Samuel Herman and Alexander Baciu, residents of Saline, Michigan, frustrated with Comcast's slow and unreliable cable internet, founded their own fiber internet service provider, Prime-One. They were driven by personal experiences of inadequate internet speeds, especially with multiple family members using the service simultaneously. Prime-One offers symmetrical gigabit speeds starting at $80 per month, with no data caps or contracts, and has so far connected 1,500 homes with plans to expand to 4,000. The company aims to provide reliable, high-speed internet in areas underserved by major providers like Comcast and Frontier. With over 100 customers already, they hope to reach a 30% subscription rate to become profitable. Despite delays in their expansion timeline, they remain committed to improving local internet options.

Lasagna Battery Cell

作者: nixass | 评分: 123 | 评论数: 10 | 发布时间: 2025-07-10 19:19
文章摘要

这篇文章警告了使用反应性炊具和食物接触可能导致的化学反应问题，特别是“千层面电池”现象。当酸性或含盐的食物（如番茄酱、腌泡汁）与不同金属（如铝箔和钢锅）接触时，可能发生电化学反应，导致铝箔腐蚀、穿孔，甚至产生有毒物质。为避免此问题，建议使用非反应性材料如不锈钢、玻璃、瓷器或搪瓷容器。在烧烤中使用“德州纸巾法”时，也应注意避免铝箔与钢制器具直接接触，推荐使用但纸、烤纸或塑料袋等材料来防止反应。文章还提到，虽然不锈钢一般不易受腐蚀，但仍需注意不同金属间的电化学反应。
