After millions of years, why are carnivorous plants still so small?

作者: gmays | 评分: 200 | 评论数: 25 | 发布时间: 2025-06-13 20:04
文章摘要

carnivorous plants have been trapping animals for millions of years, but they have never evolved to become larger. These plants, which obtain nutrients by consuming insects and other small creatures, have independently evolved at least ten times in history. Despite their ability to thrive in nutrient-poor environments, carnivorous plants remain small. This is likely due to the trade-offs between the energy invested in capturing prey and the energy required for growth and survival. The limited size may also be a result of their specialized habitat requirements and the need to maintain efficient trapping mechanisms. Overall, their small stature persists as a successful evolutionary strategy.

理查德·艾尔曼（Richard Ellmann）的《詹姆斯·乔伊斯》传记（1959年出版）以其842页的庞大篇幅和详尽的细节震撼了读者。该书生动描绘了乔伊斯的生活与创作，展现了他从酗酒、失明到创作如《尤利西斯》等文学巨作的过程。艾尔曼在1950年代通过广泛的研究和接触乔伊斯亲友，获得了大量未公开的资料，成功撰写了这部传记。他巧妙地运用事实，结合心理描写和文学批评，使该书不仅成为乔伊斯生平的权威记录，还开创了传记写作的新风格。尽管艾尔曼淡化了乔伊斯的政治兴趣，但仍展现了乔伊斯作为艺术家的全球视野和创作心理。该书被誉为传记文学的经典之作，对后世读者和学者产生了深远影响。

In praise of “normal” engineers

作者: zdw | 评分: 106 | 评论数: 25 | 发布时间: 2025-06-19 17:36
文章摘要

本文探讨了“10x工程师”概念的实际意义和局限性，强调了“普通工程师”的价值。虽然确实存在一些极为高效的工程师，但“10x工程师”常被误解为在所有方面都超越他人，这不现实。首先，生产力的衡量标准多样且复杂，不同技术领域和项目阶段的技能需求各异，且人的能力并非一成不变。其次，软件开发是团队协作的结果，个体工程师的速度不决定整体软件交付速度，团队的协作效率才是关键。单个工程师拥有软件所有权可能导致单一故障点，公司成长过程中需转向团队共同拥有和维护软件。因此，作者主张不应过度追求“10x工程师”，而应重视团队整体的建设和协作。

Revisiting Knuth's "Premature Optimization" Paper

作者: Bogdanp | 评分: 6 | 评论数: 1 | 发布时间: 2025-06-20 01:37
文章摘要

Malte Skarupke revisits Donald Knuth's famous "premature optimization is the root of all evil" quote, clarifying its common misinterpretation. Knuth's original paper actually discusses the use of goto statements in structured programming, not optimization. He illustrates the performance trade-offs when using arrays versus maps for data structures like multisets. Knuth initially uses a goto example for inserting elements into an array-based multiset, showing how it can simplify code and avoid extra checks. However, he critiques this approach as still suboptimal, proposing an "optimistic" insertion method that eliminates a condition from the loop for better efficiency. He further optimizes by unrolling the loop, demonstrating potential performance gains. Knuth's discussion highlights the balance between readability and efficiency, emphasizing that premature optimization can lead to complex code without significant performance benefits.
