NASA won't publish key climate change report online

作者: belter | 评分: 12 | 评论数: 1 | 发布时间: 2025-08-04 19:47
文章摘要

NASA has reversed its earlier indication to publish the National Climate Assessment (NCA) report on its website, citing no legal obligation to do so. This decision complicates public access to the key scientific findings on climate change impacts across the U.S. The report, typically released every four to five years, outlines climate risks to health, agriculture, and infrastructure. The White House had previously stated that NASA would host the report after the original site, globalchange.gov, was shut down. However, NASA's refusal to post the document raises concerns about transparency and government support for climate research, especially as NASA's budget faces significant cuts under the Trump administration. The next NCA is due in 2028, but its future is uncertain after many scientists were dismissed.

文章摘要

这篇文章讨论了滑雪租赁问题，这是一个经典的在线算法问题。问题场景是：你去滑雪，但不确定会滑多少天，租滑雪设备每天1单位货币，而购买则需B单位货币。目标是决定是否以及何时购买滑雪设备。 **离线最优算法**：如果提前知道滑雪天数k，当k ≥ B时应购买，k < B时应租。总费用为min{k, B}。 **简单在线算法**：不知道k时，可以先租，直到租金达到B时购买。这种方法的最坏情况成本是两倍于离线最优算法，即竞争比为2。 **随机化算法**：文章进一步探讨了一种随机化算法，通过选择适当的概率分布P(x)，使得期望竞争比接近于$\frac{e}{e-1}$。核心思想是找到一种概率分布，使得期望成本与最优成本的比值（竞争比）不随k变化，从而优化算法性能。经过推导，得出P(x)应为指数分布，满足$\int_0^\infty P(x)dx = 1$。 最终，随机化算法能提供更好的平均性能，适合多次决策场景。
