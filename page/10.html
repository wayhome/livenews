World's largest wildlife crossing reaches critical milestone. Now what?

作者: Stratoscope | 评分: 75 | 评论数: 14 | 发布时间: 2025-04-01 18:37
文章摘要

The Wallis Annenberg Wildlife Crossing in Agoura Hills reached a major milestone as 6,000 cubic yards of specially designed soil were added to its top. This soil, a mix of sand, silt, clay, compost, and local fungi, is intended to mimic native soils and support the growth of local plants. The project, expected to be completed by 2026, aims to provide a safe passage for wildlife over the 101 Freeway, connecting habitats fragmented by human development. Officials and scientists celebrated the soil addition, marking progress in Stage 1, with seeding and planting of native shrubs and perennials planned for completion this summer. Despite delays due to heavy rains, the final phase will involve covering Agoura Road with a tunnel and additional soil to blend the crossing with surrounding hills. The project also includes a nursery that has collected and nurtured one million seeds from native plants to restore the area's natural environment.

DeepSeek: Inference-Time Scaling for Generalist Reward Modeling

作者: tim_sw | 评分: 158 | 评论数: 5 | 发布时间: 2025-04-04 04:50
文章摘要
