Understanding the Origins and the Evolution of Vi and Vim

作者: amosjyng | 评分: 275 | 评论数: 24 | 发布时间: 2025-04-15 10:33
文章摘要

这篇文章回顾了Vi和Vim文本编辑器的起源与演变，它们的发展与开源软件历史紧密相连。文章提到，Vim的创始人Bram Moolenaar去世，引发了作者对Vim历史的反思。Vim的发展可追溯到1973年，英国首个UNIX系统安装在伦敦玛丽女王大学，George Coulouris教授在那里开发了名为"em"的编辑器，推动了"vi"及其他"vi克隆"的诞生。早期UNIX的"ed"编辑器因适用于电传打字机而设计，但随着显示终端普及，其单行编辑模式显得不便。Coulouris改进了"ed”创造出"em"，而UC Berkeley的Bill Joy受其启发，开发出全屏模式的"vi"编辑器。最终，"vi"从"ex"编辑器的可视模式演变而来，成为广泛使用的文本编辑器。

Why is Good Friday called Good Friday?

作者: vitoc | 评分: 113 | 评论数: 16 | 发布时间: 2025-04-18 03:59
文章摘要

Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, a central event in Christianity. Despite its somber nature, the day is called "Good" Friday because it historically meant "holy." The term reflects the day's religious significance, marking Christ's sacrifice for humanity's sins. Although a day of sorrow and reflection, it is seen as "good" due to the promise of salvation and victory over death, culminating in Easter Sunday. Globally, Good Friday is observed through various traditions. In churches, the Stations of the Cross are commemorated, and services focus on the Passion of Christ. Roman Catholics hold the Three Hours' Devotion and processions, while the Eastern Orthodox Church performs Vespers with an epitaphios. Reenactments of Christ's crucifixion, like those in Jerusalem, and customs such as making flower carpets in Central and South America, also mark the day. Ultimately, Good Friday's "goodness" lies in its spiritual significance, leading to redemption and hope.
