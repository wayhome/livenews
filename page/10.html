Hooded pitohui, one of the only toxic birds

作者: thunderbong | 评分: 6 | 评论数: 1 | 发布时间: 2025-04-24 16:17
文章摘要

The hooded pitohui, found in Papua New Guinea, is one of the world's only known toxic birds. Its feathers contain batrachotoxins, potent neurotoxic alkaloids also found in South America's poison dart frogs. Ornithologist Jack Dumbacher discovered this in 1989 when handling the birds caused his hands to tingle and his mouth to burn. Later research revealed that the toxin comes from the pitohui's diet of melyrid beetles. Why this bird consumes such toxic prey, despite having other defenses, remains a mystery. This trait is rare among birds, making the hooded pitohui a unique case in the animal kingdom.

文章摘要

这篇文章主要讨论了MOS 6502处理器的非法操作码（undefined opcodes）的来源和工作原理。6502处理器使用了一个130×21位的解码ROM（PLA）来解释指令，而不是使用微代码。文章详细介绍了PLA如何通过匹配操作码和时钟周期来触发特定操作。例如，分支指令的编码方式使得多个相似操作码可以共享相同的解码线路。非法操作码如"LAX absolute"（$AF）由于与合法操作码（如"LDA absolute"和"LDX absolute"）编码相似，也会触发相同的操作。文章通过具体例子展示了PLA线路如何在不同周期内执行内存读取、寄存器加载等操作，解释了非法操作码如何产生意外但有用的效果。
