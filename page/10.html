An Interview with Zen Chief Architect Mike Clark

作者: Smaug123 | 评分: 144 | 评论数: 4 | 发布时间: 2025-03-24 19:05
文章摘要

这篇文章是Casey Muratori对Zen架构首席设计师Mike Clark的采访总结。Zen架构是x86生态系统中极为重要的微架构，帮助AMD在CPU市场份额上从10%提升至25%。采访中，Mike Clark讨论了x64指令集架构（ISA）在低功耗设计中的表现，指出x64和ARM在功耗和性能上并无根本差异，主要是市场需求不同导致优化方向不同。对于x64的4k页大小限制问题，Clark表示更大页尺寸有助于提高TLB效率，但4k到2mb的直接跳跃过大，可能需要中间尺寸。整体而言，Clark强调微架构中的各种差异更多是设计上的取舍，而非根本性问题。

What happens to DNA data of millions as 23andMe files bankruptcy?

作者: ajdude | 评分: 18 | 评论数: 3 | 发布时间: 2025-03-27 19:39
文章摘要

23andMe, a pioneer in DNA testing, has filed for bankruptcy, raising concerns about the fate of its users' genetic data. The company, which has over 15 million users, faced backlash after a 2023 data breach. Experts worry that the sale of the company could put sensitive genetic information at risk. Although some U.S. states have privacy laws allowing users to delete their data, the company's privacy policy previously permitted the sharing and use of personal information, including genetic and non-genetic data. Consumers are advised to delete their data from 23andMe's website to protect their privacy. The situation also highlights broader concerns about the safety of genetic data held by other DNA testing companies, as such data could potentially be acquired by entities like insurance companies, affecting coverage and premiums.
