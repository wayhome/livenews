Dead trees keep surprisingly large amounts of carbon out of atmosphere

作者: PaulHoule | 评分: 70 | 评论数: 14 | 发布时间: 2025-04-14 14:23
文章摘要

美国佛蒙特大学的研究发现，倒伏在溪流中的大型死树能够储存大量碳，起到减缓气候变化的作用。这些死树由于较低的外露表面积，减少了分解作用，从而缓慢释放碳。研究显示，原始森林中溪流内的碳储存量比成熟森林高出四到五倍，且这种碳池随时间增长。尽管上游溪流占地面积小，但其碳储存效应显著。研究强调了森林与溪流间的动态关系，指出大尺寸的倒木不仅能横跨小溪形成持久的水坝，还不断积累有机物，增强碳储存效果。这项研究揭示了先前未被充分量化的碳储存形式。

Censors Ignore Unencrypted HTTP/2 Traffic (2024)

作者: ArinaS | 评分: 58 | 评论数: 7 | 发布时间: 2025-04-14 18:23
文章摘要

文章主要探讨了使用未加密的HTTP/2协议绕过 censorship 的可能性。研究发现，在中国和伊朗，未加密的HTTP/2流量未被审查，可通过此方法访问被封锁的网站。尽管没有任何浏览器直接支持未加密的HTTP/2，研究检测到最多6.28%的网站支持这种协议。文章介绍了两种建立HTTP/2连接的方法：直接使用HTTP/2（Prior Knowledge）和通过HTTP/1.1升级（Upgrade Mechanism）。由于HTTP/2采用二进制格式，与文本格式的HTTP/1.1相比，解析难度增加，导致审查系统未能有效拦截。文章还提供了一种工具来检测网站是否支持未加密的HTTP/2。然而，作者提醒不要通过未加密的HTTP/2传输敏感数据，因为存在被窃听的风险。这一发现为规避网络审查提供了新的技术手段。

文章摘要

James Watson attributed the start of his Nobel Prize journey to a 1951 conference in Naples, where he met Maurice Wilkins, with whom he and Francis Crick later shared the Nobel Prize for discovering the DNA double helix. Watson, then a 22-year-old postdoc, was arrogant and competitive. At the conference, he was unimpressed by most presentations but was intrigued by Wilkins' X-ray images of DNA. Despite his attempts to connect with Wilkins, their interactions were awkward. Nonetheless, this encounter fueled Watson's determination to uncover DNA's structure. He eventually teamed up with Crick at Cambridge, leading to their groundbreaking model of DNA. Howard Markel's book *The Secret of Life* reveals behind-the-scenes drama, including Watson's dismissive attitude toward Barbara Wright and his rivalry with Wilkins and Rosalind Franklin. Markel highlights Watson's unfair portrayal of Franklin in his 1968 book *The Double Helix*, depicting her as a temperamental figure, despite her crucial contributions to the DNA research. Franklin, a skilled crystallographer, faced professional tension with Wilkins, who viewed her as an assistant rather than an independent scientist. Markel aims to give Franklin proper recognition for her role in the DNA discovery.
