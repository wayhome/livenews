Show HN: AG-UI Protocol – Bring Agents into Frontend Applications

文章摘要

AG-UI 是一个开源、轻量级、基于事件的协议，旨在标准化 AI 代理与前端应用程序的交互。它定义了16种标准事件类型，支持多种事件传输方式（如SSE、WebSockets等），并提供了灵活的中间件层以确保跨环境兼容性。AG-UI 允许双向状态同步、实时上下文增强、生成式 UI 及更多功能，兼容多种流行代理框架如 LangGraph、Mastra、CrewAI 等。该协议提供了参考实现和客户端库，帮助开发者快速构建支持代理功能的应用。AG-UI 通过与社区和多个框架合作，确保其实用性和广泛适用性。更多信息和示例可在 ag-ui.com 查看。

Noncoders using AI to prompt their ideas into reality. It's called 'vibe coding'

作者: Kerrick | 评分: 4 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-05-14 01:29
文章摘要

"Vibe coding" is a new trend where people use generative AI, like ChatGPT, to create apps and software with plain English instead of traditional coding. OpenAI co-founder Andrej Karpathy popularized the term, describing it as coding by "giving in to the vibes" and letting AI handle the technical details. AI-powered platforms such as Cursor and Replit enable even those without technical backgrounds to build apps. For example, Nadia Ben Brahim Maazaoui used ChatGPT to create a personalized "robot friend" for her daughter, helping with tasks like storytelling and teaching English. However, these AI tools often produce error-laden outputs, requiring users to either fix the code or keep requesting adjustments. Despite challenges, "vibe coding" has democratized software development, attracting novices and children like 8-year-old Fay Robinett, who builds apps as a hobby. This trend has fostered online communities where vibe coders share projects and tips.
