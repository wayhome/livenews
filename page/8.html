OpenVertebrate Presents a Database of 13K 3D Scans of Specimens

作者: exikyut | 评分: 177 | 评论数: 14 | 发布时间: 2025-04-05 02:15
文章摘要

The Florida Museum of Natural History has launched the *openVertebrate* (oVert) project, offering over 13,000 free 3D scans of vertebrate specimens. These high-resolution CT scans, taken from 2017 to 2023, cover more than half the genera of amphibians, reptiles, fishes, and mammals, focusing primarily on skeletal structures but also including some soft tissue visualizations. The project aims to provide researchers, educators, students, and the public with detailed anatomical data that would otherwise require destructive dissection. Plans are underway to expand the collection to 20,000 specimens, with digital images and 3D files available for download and 3D printing.

文章摘要
