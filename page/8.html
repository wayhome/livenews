macOS Command tools, threads and QoS

作者: zdw | 评分: 9 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-09-10 18:04
文章摘要

本文解释了为何在配备Apple Silicon芯片的Mac上，使用`tar`和Gzip压缩大文件夹时会很慢。原因并非任务被分配到效率核心（E cores），而是`tar`命令本身是单线程的，无法充分利用性能核心（P cores）的多线程优势。macOS会根据线程的QoS（服务质量）级别分配核心，高QoS任务通常分配到P核心，低QoS任务分配到E核心。通过Activity Monitor可以查看核心使用情况，建议使用支持多线程的替代工具来提高压缩速度。

For example, you might sanitize a filename by removing slashes, but you wouldn't "encode" the slashes - you'd just remove them. [security_guru]: Both approaches have their place. Encoding is usually safer since it preserves data integrity, but sanitization is needed when dealing with formats that don't have proper encoding mechanisms (like filenames) or when you want to enforce stricter content policies (e.g. removing ALL HTML tags from user comments).
