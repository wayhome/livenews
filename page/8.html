文章摘要

CERN has developed a portable antimatter containment device to overcome the interference caused by its current hardware, which limits measurement precision. The new device, two meters long, uses superconducting magnets and requires an extreme vacuum and significant power supply, including liquid helium. It was successfully tested with protons, demonstrating no particle loss during transport. The device was moved around the CERN campus, confirming its stability and ability to maintain low temperatures. CERN aims to use this container to ship antimatter to other labs, including a future facility in Düsseldorf, Germany, potentially improving measurement precision by over 100 times. The main challenge is managing the liquid helium supply during transport.
