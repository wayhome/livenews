文章摘要

The Tea app, a women-only dating safety platform, has suffered a major data breach, with over 59 GB of data exposed, including 72,000 images and 1.1 million private messages. Initially, an unsecured Firebase storage bucket was exploited, leaking driver's licenses, selfies, and photos. Despite securing the bucket, hackers shared the data on hacking forums and created a site to publicly rate exposed selfies. A second database, containing private messages from 2023 to 2025, including sensitive topics, was also found. The breach affects users who signed up before 2024. Tea confirmed the incident, stating they are working with cybersecurity experts and law enforcement to investigate and contain the breach. The exposed data raises concerns about user privacy and potential social engineering attacks.
