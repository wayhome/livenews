本文介绍了一种称为“黑暗镜像”（Dark Mirror）意识形态的现象。这种意识形态与其对立的“光明镜像”（Light Mirror）意识形态在事实主张上完全一致，唯一的区别在于道德立场和对行动方案的分歧。文章指出，这种意识形态在现实中非常罕见，但在网络政治辩论中经常被提及。作者以最低工资为例，说明持相反立场的人往往指责对方明知事实却故意作恶。文章还以达斯·维达和撒旦为例，说明“黑暗镜像”信仰者接受与其对手相同的宇宙论或事实主张，但选择用于邪恶目的。真正的“黑暗基督徒”极为罕见，除了撒旦及其恶魔，几乎没有人符合这种严格定义。总之，“黑暗镜像”意识形态在道德上与对立面完全相反，但在事实主张上保持一致。

评论摘要

Z-Wave is remaking itself into an open source protocol

作者: elsewhen | 评分: 34 | 评论数: 1 | 发布时间: 2025-04-06 22:38
文章摘要

Z-Wave is reinventing itself to stay relevant in the smart home market. Once a closed protocol, it's now open source and introducing Z-Wave Long Range (ZWLR) technology to extend device range up to 1.5 miles, improving connectivity for locks, lights, and sensors. Despite its widespread use in home security systems, Z-Wave has faced challenges due to its reliance on hubs and competition from other protocols like Matter. To ensure its survival, the Z-Wave Alliance is working to integrate Z-Wave devices with Matter. ZWLR aims to address Z-Wave's previous limitations by allowing devices to connect directly to the hub, rather than through a mesh network, enhancing reliability and ease of use.
