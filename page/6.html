Gmail Users Can Send Encrypted Email to Anyone

作者: taubek | 评分: 5 | 评论数: 2 | 发布时间: 2025-04-02 20:28
文章摘要

Gmail has introduced end-to-end encryption (E2EE) on its 21st anniversary, addressing long-standing security concerns. With 2.5 billion users, Gmail has been a prime target for cyberattacks, prompting Google to develop a new encryption method. This innovation eliminates complex certificate requirements and allows users to send encrypted emails to any email platform. Initially, the feature will be available for enterprise users to send E2EE emails within their organization, with plans to extend it to all Gmail users and other email platforms in the future. The encryption uses customer-controlled keys, enhancing privacy by ensuring even Google servers cannot access the content. Non-Gmail recipients will view emails via a restricted Gmail interface. This development marks a significant advancement in Gmail's security framework.
