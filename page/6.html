The Humble Programmer (1972)

作者: squircle | 评分: 103 | 评论数: 19 | 发布时间: 2025-06-17 01:25
文章摘要

Edsger W. Dijkstra's "The Humble Programmer" reflects on the slow emergence of programming as a recognized profession in the early 1950s in the Netherlands. Initially, programming was not seen as an intellectually respectable discipline, lacking a solid theoretical foundation. Dijkstra recounts a pivotal conversation with his boss, A. van Wijngaarden, who encouraged him to help establish programming as a respected field. This conversation shifted Dijkstra's career path from theoretical physics to programming. He also humorously notes that when he married in 1957, the authorities did not recognize "programmer" as a valid profession. Dijkstra describes the early days of computing when computers were unique, experimental machines, and the focus was on hardware. Programmers were largely overlooked, as attention centered on keeping the machines running. The essay highlights how past perceptions still influence misunderstandings about programming today.
