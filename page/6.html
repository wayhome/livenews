Mott Problem

作者: niklasbuschmann | 评分: 4 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-07-04 16:34
文章摘要

The Mott problem is a key question in quantum mechanics: how a spherically symmetric wave function results in linear tracks observed in a cloud chamber. First raised by Albert Einstein and Max Born in 1927, it was solved by Nevill Francis Mott in 1929. Mott's solution, which does not rely on wavefunction collapse, is an early example of decoherence theory. In high-energy physics, spherical wave functions typically describe particle states, yet detectors like cloud chambers show linear tracks. Mott's analysis showed that even with a spherical starting wave, interactions with the environment (like atoms in the chamber) lead to the appearance of linear tracks due to the alpha particle's high velocity and successive atomic excitations. This resolves the paradox by explaining how wave symmetry translates into observed linear particle paths.

AV1@Scale: Film Grain Synthesis, The Awakening

作者: CharlesW | 评分: 105 | 评论数: 23 | 发布时间: 2025-07-03 16:34
文章摘要

Netflix最新博文介绍了AV1编解码器中的胶片颗粒合成（Film Grain Synthesis, FGS）技术，该技术旨在优化流媒体视频的视觉质量和压缩效率。胶片颗粒为影片增添了真实感和怀旧感，但其随机性给视频压缩带来了挑战。AV1通过使用自回归模型（AR模型）捕捉颗粒图案，并通过缩放函数调整颗粒强度，从而在保留艺术完整性的同时实现高效压缩。FGS技术首先对视频进行去噪，然后压缩视频，并在播放时重新合成胶片颗粒。这不仅提升了画质，还显著减少了比特率，为用户提供了更流畅的高质量观影体验。

评论摘要

文章摘要
