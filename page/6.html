This event happens monthly since September 2023 and will continue until July 2029, though visibility varies by location. On July 20, 2025, the eclipse will be visible in parts of the U.S. and Canada in the early hours before dawn, with optimal viewing towards the eastern horizon away from city lights. No special equipment is needed, though darker locations provide better views. The Pleiades, also known as Messier 45, contain over a thousand young stars bound by gravity and are best observed from the Northern Hemisphere between October and April. Other celestial events in July 2025 include Venus near the crescent moon and Mars spotted next to the moon.

Major rule about cooking meat turns out to be wrong

作者: voxadam | 评分: 207 | 评论数: 32 | 发布时间: 2025-07-23 19:02
文章摘要
