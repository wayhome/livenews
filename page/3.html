James Webb Space Telescope Reveals That Most Galaxies Rotate Clockwise

作者: instagraham | 评分: 87 | 评论数: 21 | 发布时间: 2025-03-31 10:31
文章摘要

NASA’s James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) has observed that most spiral galaxies rotate in the same direction, with about two-thirds of 263 studied galaxies rotating clockwise. This challenges the idea of an isotropic universe, where galaxy rotations should be evenly distributed. Lior Shamir, the sole author of the study published in *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society*, suggests this asymmetry could be due to the universe's origins in a rotational state, possibly supporting black hole cosmology—the theory that our universe exists within a black hole. Another explanation could be the Doppler shift effect, making galaxies rotating opposite to the Milky Way appear brighter and more detectable. Further research is needed to confirm these findings and their implications for cosmology, including potential recalibrations of distance measurements in the universe.
