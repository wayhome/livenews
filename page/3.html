EE 2021, 16(2): 1–13 # 9. Conclusions The results of the study clearly indicate that all participants, both students and teachers, expressed a positive attitude towards the implementation of the flipped classroom model in learning English, particularly in the realm of online learning. The implementation of the flipped classroom model is perceived as beneficial in fostering a more dynamic and engaging learning environment. The data analysis reveals that students exhibit a favorable response to this learning model, while teachers also express positive views regarding its implementation. However, it's crucial to acknowledge that the study has its limitations. The research was conducted solely within a single school, specifically at SMP Muhammadiyah 1 Surakarta, with a limited number of participants, consisting of 30 students and 3 teachers. Additionally, the study focused exclusively on the implementation of the flipped classroom model in English classes, particularly in online learning. These limitations highlight the need for further research to explore the broader applicability of the flipped classroom model in various educational settings and subjects, involving a more extensive and diverse participant pool. Future studies could delve into different educational levels, subjects, and learning environments to provide a more comprehensive understanding of the model's effectiveness across diverse contexts. This would contribute to a more nuanced and robust comprehension of the flipped classroom's impact on learning outcomes and student engagement.

Reproducible C++ builds by logging Git hashes

作者: j4cobgarby | 评分: 21 | 评论数: 7 | 发布时间: 2025-11-14 09:45
文章摘要

本文介绍了如何在C++项目中通过记录Git提交哈希来实现可复现的构建。作者在快速开发过程中经常遇到难以确定哪个代码版本生成了特定输出文件的问题。解决方案是在构建时通过脚本自动生成包含当前Git提交哈希的C++头文件，并将其编译进程序。具体实现包括：编写bash脚本获取Git哈希并生成头文件，配置CMake在每次编译时运行该脚本，并在代码中引用该哈希值输出到日志文件。此外，作者提醒此方法仅适用于已提交的代码，未提交的修改会导致混淆。这种方法简单有效地解决了版本追踪问题。

Comparing Integers and Doubles

作者: pfent | 评分: 5 | 评论数: 2 | 发布时间: 2025-11-12 18:16
文章摘要

文章揭示了在数据库系统（如PostgreSQL）中，当比较大整数和双精度浮点数时，由于双精度浮点数的精度限制（52位尾数），会导致比较结果不传递的问题（a=b且a=c但b≠c）。这会影响查询优化器和哈希连接的正确性。作者提出了一种正确的比较方法：先比较双精度转换结果，如果不相等则直接比较，否则将双精度数转回整数再比较，并处理可能的精度损失。目前只有Python和SQLite实现了正确的比较逻辑，而大多数编程语言和数据库系统都存在此问题。作者提供了C++实现代码供参考。

评论摘要
主要讨论点：[浮点数比较的合理方式] 不同观点： • [第一种观点：认为浮点数运算不符合数学性质] - 论据：浮点数运算不满足结合律、交换律、部分序、弱序、对称性、自反性、反对称性等数学性质。 - 例子：浮点数加法不满足结合律，例如 `(1.0 + 2.0) + 3.0` 可能与 `1.0 + (2.0 + 3.0)` 结果不同。 - 结论：唯一合理的比较方式是将有理数转换为字符串的十进制展开形式。 • [第二种观点：认为可以通过学习浮点数比较的正确方法] - 论据：浮点数比较有其特定的规则和方法，可以通过学习掌握。 - 例子：使用误差范围（epsilon）进行比较，而不是直接比较。 - 结论：浮点数比较是可行的，但需要理解其特性和限制。 补充讨论： - 争议焦点：浮点数比较是否可以通过学习正确方法解决，还是必须依赖字符串转换。 - 讨论关系：第二种观点是对第一种观点的反驳，认为浮点数比较问题可以通过技术手段解决，而非完全否定其可行性。

> 2024-02-23 1. I have a question. If someone is in prison, and they don’t have any money and can’t afford a lawyer, do they get a free lawyer? Like, if they can’t afford one, do they get a free one? Or do they not get a lawyer at all if they can’t afford one? I’m just wondering how that works. In the United States, individuals who are charged with a crime and cannot afford legal representation are entitled to legal counsel under the Sixth Amendment of the U.S. Constitution, as established by the landmark Supreme Court case *Gideon v. Wainwright* (1963). This means that if someone is facing criminal charges and cannot afford an attorney, the court will appoint a public defender or a court-appointed attorney to represent them at no cost. Public defenders are government-funded lawyers who provide legal representation to defendants who cannot afford private attorneys. However, the quality and availability of public defenders can vary significantly depending on the jurisdiction. Some public defenders may have heavy caseloads, which can limit the amount of time they can dedicate to each case. Despite this, they are still obligated to provide competent legal representation. In some cases, if a conflict of interest arises (e.g., multiple defendants in the same case), the court may appoint a private attorney who will be compensated by the state to represent the defendant. It's worth noting that this right to counsel generally applies to criminal cases, not civil cases (e.g., personal injury, divorce, etc.), though there are some limited circumstances where legal aid may be available for certain civil matters. So, to answer your question: Yes, if someone is in prison and cannot afford a lawyer for their criminal case, they are entitled to a free lawyer provided by the state.
