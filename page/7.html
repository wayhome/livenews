Largest punk archive to find new home at MTSU's Center for Popular Music

作者: gnabgib | 评分: 70 | 评论数: 6 | 发布时间: 2025-06-02 22:29
文章摘要

Middle Tennessee State University’s Center for Popular Music will house the world’s largest punk archive, the Maximum Rocknroll (MRR) collection, which includes 60,000 vinyl records, photos, zines, and documents from global punk history. Weighing eight tons, this iconic collection from the Bay Area will be added to the center’s over one million music memorabilia items. MTSU’s center, one of the oldest and largest research facilities for American folk and popular music, aims to become the new hub for punk research. The archive, expected to arrive in June, will be cataloged for online access and is set to attract a broader audience, offering opportunities for research, events, and outreach like listening parties and zine workshops. This acquisition not only preserves punk history but also amplifies it for future generations.

How to Store Data on Paper?

作者: mofosyne | 评分: 110 | 评论数: 24 | 发布时间: 2025-05-31 07:20
文章摘要

文章介绍了如何将数字数据存储在纸上，即“纸质数据存储”。作者探讨了几种方法，包括打印可执行程序（使用base64编码）、秘密消息（使用GPG-AES256加密并编码为QR码）、声音片段（使用Optar编码为MP3）和压缩的科学论文（使用颜色安全编码的PDF）。关键在于将数据转换为特定编码的图像，并通过软件进行编码和解码。 字符编码方式，如base16、base32和base64，通过OCR识别，但容量和识别率受字体大小和类型影响。Base64理论上可达17KB每页，但实际OCR识别有限。作者还发明了优化OCR的bocr32编码。 点阵编码方式，如QR码和Optar，容量高达70-100KB每页，依赖于打印和扫描分辨率。彩色点阵编码虽美观，但尚未深入研究。 总体而言，不同编码方式各有优缺点，选择取决于具体需求和设备条件。
