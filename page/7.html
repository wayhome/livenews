Everything’s a bug (or an issue)

作者: dboreham | 评分: 56 | 评论数: 12 | 发布时间: 2025-05-19 03:46
文章摘要

文章《Everything’s a bug (or an issue)》由David Boreham撰写，讲述了一种高效、可靠且令人愉快的软件项目管理方法。作者通过回顾在硅谷一家类似FAANG公司的经历，描述了一种以“bug council”为核心的工作方式。团队定期开会，按照优先级逐一讨论和更新bug状态，确保所有人同步。 该方法基于四个关键原则： 1. **所有任务皆为bug**：无论是新功能、文档缺失还是性能问题，都视为bug录入系统。 2. **统一且固定的bug记录模式**：通过一致的模式捕捉bug的状态、优先级和相互依赖关系，确保清晰和具体。 3. **单一责任制**：每个bug仅分配给一个人，确保责任明确，便于项目管理。 4. **强大的查询功能**：通过可保存和共享的查询视图，用户可以根据需求定制bug列表。 这种系统最初称为BugSplat，后来演变为Bugzilla，通过这些原则有效管理项目，确保软件质量。

Why does the Beetham Tower hum?

作者: thunderbong | 评分: 7 | 评论数: 2 | 发布时间: 2025-05-19 12:13
文章摘要

Beetham Tower in Manchester emits a loud humming noise during windy conditions due to the interaction between wind and the glass sculpture at its top. The wind passing over the edges of the glass panes creates turbulence, which is then amplified through resonance, particularly between the glass panes. This resonance can produce sound levels similar to a tenor saxophone, often compared to an alien spacecraft. Efforts to reduce the hum have included attaching aluminum profiles to disrupt turbulence, but the problem persists at high wind speeds. Solving the issue may require further modifications to the glass structure to dampen resonance, though such acoustic problems are notoriously difficult to resolve.
