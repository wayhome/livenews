文章摘要

NASA's Psyche spacecraft, en route to a metal-rich asteroid, has halted its engines due to a propulsion system issue. The problem involves a drop in xenon fuel pressure in the line feeding the spacecraft's plasma thrusters, which are essential for its solar electric propulsion system. Although there is no immediate impact on the spacecraft's trajectory, engineers are investigating the issue and have decided to keep the engines off until they understand the cause. If necessary, they can switch to a backup fuel line. The mission, costing over $1.4 billion, launched in October 2023 and aims to reach the asteroid Psyche in 2029, providing valuable data on metallic asteroids.

Chain of Recursive Thoughts: Make AI think harder by making it argue with itself

作者: miles | 评分: 521 | 评论数: 75 | 发布时间: 2025-04-29 17:19
文章摘要

这篇文章介绍了一种名为CoRT（Chain of Recursive Thoughts）的方法，通过让AI反复自我辩论来提升其表现。CoRT使AI模型递归地思考其回应，生成替代方案并选择最佳答案，类似于让AI自我怀疑并不断尝试。实验显示，该方法显著提高了Mistral 3.1 24B模型在编程任务中的表现。其工作原理是AI生成初始回应，决定思考轮数，每轮生成三个替代回应并评估选择最佳结果。文章还提供了使用Web UI的指南和代码示例，并说明了该方法的关键在于自我评估、竞争性替代生成、迭代优化和动态思考深度。项目采用MIT许可证。

How the Internet Left 4chan Behind

作者: FinnLobsien | 评分: 31 | 评论数: 7 | 发布时间: 2025-04-30 10:44
文章摘要

评论摘要
