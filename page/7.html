AMD's Magny Cours and HyperTransport Interconnect

作者: brian_herman | 评分: 5 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-07-12 01:10
文章摘要

AMD的Magny Cours（Opteron 6000系列）是2010年前提高核心数量的一次尝试。该处理器通过将两个Phenom II X6的6核芯片通过HyperTransport（HT）链路连接在一起，实现了12核设计，避免了重新开发新芯片的复杂性。每个芯片有自己的内存控制器，形成NUMA架构，但也支持内存交错以提高性能。芯片内HT链路为12.8 GB/s，外部提供四个HT端口用于多路系统连接。在双路系统中，跨节点内存延迟为120-130纳秒，低于现代系统，显示出当时较优的延迟表现。内存控制器负责缓存一致性，可选择使用HT assist功能来减少探听流量，提升效率。

The Ghost of Muriel Spark

作者: Caiero | 评分: 3 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-07-09 18:38
文章摘要

Muriel Spark's 1992 memoir *Curriculum Vitae* concludes in 1957 with her first novel, *The Comforters*. By then, aged 74, Spark was contemplating endings and chose Martin Stannard to write her biography. Known for her mastery of beginnings and endings in works like *The Girls of Slender Means* and *The Prime of Miss Jean Brodie*, Spark invited Stannard after reviewing his biography of Evelyn Waugh. A meticulous hoarder, she had preserved four decades of her life in extensive archives, which now reside in the National Library of Scotland and the University of Tulsa. Before her novelistic success, Spark was a biographer, starting with *Child of Light* (1951), a life of Mary Shelley. Born in Edinburgh in 1918, she married young and moved to Southern Rhodesia, but returned to England in 1944. After working in propaganda and leading the Poetry Society, she became a biographer and critic, facing financial struggles until her conversion to Catholicism and the success of *The Comforters* in 1954. Spark believed a biographer’s task was to summarize facts, an art she perfected in her own works. Her novels, often distilled from extensive research, reflected her skill in crafting concise, elegant narratives. Ghosts and hauntings frequently appeared in her fiction, mirroring her own engagement with mortality and legacy.
