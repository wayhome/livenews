Why is there a "small house" in IBM's Code page 437?

文章摘要

这篇文章探讨了IBM PC的Code Page 437字符集中位于0x7F位置的“删除”（DEL）字符为何被显示为一个小房子（⌂）。在ASCII标准中，DEL字符本应用于删除不需要的字符，但没有明确的视觉表示。IBM PC的设计者们在一次短暂的飞机旅行中快速决定将这些控制字符赋予有趣的图形，包括笑脸、扑克牌花色、音符等，以用于文本游戏。因此，DEL字符也被归入这些“非严肃”字符之列，显示为一个小房子。这种做法背离了传统标准，如ANSI X3.32-1973，但使得字符集更具趣味性和实用性，适应了当时个人电脑的需求。

How to repair the parts that explode in Lenovo Yoga laptops (2019)

作者: feross | 评分: 46 | 评论数: 9 | 发布时间: 2025-04-13 03:32
文章摘要

Lenovo Yoga laptops from the 900 series, including 920, 930, and 940, have a design defect causing power circuitry to self-destruct, specifically a power MOSFET (Q5) that controls the audio circuit. This results in broken audio or worse issues. Lenovo hasn't acknowledged the defect or offered sufficient support, especially since failures often occur just after the warranty expires. Replacement parts are scarce and expensive, with some components undocumented and unavailable from standard suppliers. The author repaired the issue themselves by replacing the blown transistor using specialized equipment, recommending a low-cost MOSFET and applying a silicone heat transfer pad to prevent overheating. Despite the repair, other issues like USB-C port failure remain unresolved. The author advises against purchasing Yoga laptops due to these unresolved defects and Lenovo's lack of support.
