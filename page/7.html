文章摘要

本文介绍了一种新的椭圆曲线"Eccfrog512ck2"，它基于512位Weierstrass形式，提供了256位安全强度，并在性能上优于NIST P-521曲线。该曲线具有侧信道攻击抵抗能力，并避免了如MOV攻击等弱点。实验显示，Eccfrog512ck2在标量乘法上速度提升61.5%，在点生成上速度提升33.3%。该曲线适用于需要高安全性和高性能的密钥交换和数字签名应用。

文章摘要

Google is rolling out a silent update to Android devices via Google Play Services (v25.14), which includes an auto-restart feature for locked devices left inactive for three consecutive days. This update enhances security by rebooting the device, making it harder for unauthorized parties, including law enforcement, to access personal data.
