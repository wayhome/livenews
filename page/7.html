文章摘要

Pakistan is experiencing a rapid solar energy revolution, importing 17 gigawatts of solar panels in 2024, making it the world’s third-largest importer. Despite economic instability and poverty, the country’s solar boom is driven by affordable Chinese panels and high electricity costs. Unlike other nations, Pakistan’s shift is grassroots-led, not spurred by large government subsidies or major solar farms. The surge is attributed to market dynamics and people's financial need for cheaper energy, especially amid frequent blackouts and rising electricity prices. Solar adoption offers hope for coping with severe heatwaves, providing affordable cooling solutions. While the government highlights policies like zero tax on solar panels, analysts emphasize this revolution is bottom-up, driven by public demand reshaping the energy landscape.

I Found Malware in a BeamNG Mod

文章摘要

文章介绍了Owen Le Blanc及其创建的第一个Linux发行版MCC Interim Linux。1992年初，Le Blanc在曼彻斯特计算中心（MCC）工作时开发了这一发行版，旨在简化Linux的安装过程。当时，Linux内核虽已发布，但缺乏易用的安装工具，Le Blanc利用了Ted Ts'o的ramdisk代码和H. J.
