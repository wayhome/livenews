Show HN: Building a Production Finance Model for Open Source

作者: positron26 | 评分: 3 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-07-30 01:21
文章摘要

Positron is developing PrizeForge, an open-source production finance model using Elastic Fund Matching. This model empowers users, allowing programmers to focus on coding rather than fundraising. Funds are matched weekly, creating recurring revenue streams with dynamic thresholds. The process involves enrolling funds, matching them in power-of-two fragments, and triggering payouts when lower levels reach their thresholds. This approach ensures large contributions are supported by matching smaller ones, maintaining equity. PrizeForge aims to fund specific goals like Linux Desktop Environments or Rust language improvements. Initially, funds will improve the platform and support community features. For now, communication relies on third-party platforms like Reddit. The team seeks community involvement and plans to expand beyond software, reinvesting all profits into the company.

Truchet tiles是用于信息可视化和图形设计的方形瓷砖，其图案不具有旋转对称性。最早由Sébastien Truchet在1704年提出，后由Cyril Stanley Smith在1987年推广。这些瓷砖可以通过不同方向的排列形成多种图案，常用于可视化信息。典型样式包括对角线分割的对比三角形和四分之一圆弧。Truchet tiles也被用于抽象策略游戏和生成迷宫图案，其连通性可通过渗透理论分析。此外，它们在计算机图形学和各种拼贴问题中也有应用。
