文章摘要

In the movie *Trading Places*, Louis Winthorpe and Billy Ray Valentine enact a plan to get rich by outsmarting the wealthy Duke brothers. The Dukes try to profit from insider information about the orange crop report, but Winthorpe and Valentine steal the real report and create a fake one. They trick the Dukes into believing the orange crop is bad, causing them to buy large amounts of orange juice futures, driving up the price. Winthorpe then sells futures contracts at a high price. When the true report is revealed, the price of orange juice plummets, and Winthorpe and Valentine buy back the contracts at a low price, making a huge profit.
