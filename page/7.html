Private Welsh island with 19th century fort goes on the market

作者: makaimc | 评分: 50 | 评论数: 18 | 发布时间: 2025-08-09 12:25
文章摘要

Thorne Island, a 2.49-acre private island off the coast of Pembrokeshire, Wales, featuring a 19th-century fort, is on sale for £3 million (US$4 million). Originally built to defend against French invasion, the fort has been restored into a luxurious, self-sufficient property with five bedrooms, a helipad, rooftop bar, and sea-view office. British tech entrepreneur Mike Conner purchased the island in 2017 for £500,000 and spent over £2 million renovating it, including installing renewable energy systems. Accessible only by sea or air, the fort can accommodate up to 20 people and is being marketed as an ideal retreat or private getaway. Conner, who views the project as a "mid-life crisis" challenge, is now seeking a new project.
