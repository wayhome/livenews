iOS Kindle app now has a ‘get book’ button after changes to App Store rules

作者: diversion | 评分: 172 | 评论数: 10 | 发布时间: 2025-05-06 20:41
文章摘要

Amazon has updated its Kindle iOS app to include a "Get Book" button, allowing users to purchase ebooks directly from the app. This change comes after a recent ruling in the Epic Games v. Apple case, which prevents Apple from collecting a 27% commission on purchases made outside of apps or restricting alternative payment methods. Previously, due to Apple's policies, users had to purchase ebooks through a web browser and then sync them to the app. The new "Get Book" button redirects users to complete purchases via a mobile web browser, streamlining the process. However, if Apple wins its appeal, Amazon may need to revert these changes.
