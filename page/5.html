Google is asking the court for an emergency stay following Epic’s big win

作者: saikatsg | 评分: 16 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-08-01 19:39
文章摘要

Google has 14 days to make significant changes to its Play Store and business practices following its antitrust loss to Epic, unless it secures an emergency stay from the court. The required changes include ending the mandate for app developers to use Google Play Billing, allowing them to direct users to alternative payment methods, and permitting them to link to external app downloads. Google must also stop offering incentives for preinstalling its apps and work on a system to resolve disputes with Epic regarding rival app stores. However, it won't yet have to host rival stores like Epic's within Google Play, as that change is delayed until at least 2026. Google argues these rapid changes pose significant risks to its ecosystem and users.
