Helsinki records zero traffic deaths for full year

作者: DaveZale | 评分: 1105 | 评论数: 28 | 发布时间: 2025-07-30 16:08
文章摘要

赫尔辛基实现了一整年无交通死亡事故的里程碑。这一成就归功于长期规划、基础设施改造、降低限速以及加强交通执法。目前，超过一半的街道限速为30公里/小时，学校周边区域的限速也已进一步降低。街道设计优化、行人和自行车基础设施的改善以及自动化超速监控和警力巡逻的加强也发挥了重要作用。与20世纪80年代末每年近1000起事故相比，2024至2025年度仅发生277起受伤事故。尽管电动滑板车带来一些挑战，但通过调整停车规则和限速等措施已有所缓解。赫尔辛基的交通安全战略以2050年消除所有交通死亡为目标，持续推进各项安全措施。

文章摘要

Christina Chapman, a woman from Arizona and Minnesota, was sentenced to eight years in prison for her role in a North Korean remote-work scam. The scheme involved hiring North Korean IT workers who posed as U.S. citizens using stolen identities, amassing millions to allegedly support North Korea's nuclear program. Chapman managed "laptop farms," organizing and storing laptops, validating fake identities, and facilitating remote work for North Koreans at hundreds of U.S. companies, including major firms like Nike. Despite knowing the illegality, she helped the workers bypass security measures and transferred their paychecks.
