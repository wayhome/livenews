Google reverses course after blocking Nextcloud Files app

作者: bundie | 评分: 36 | 评论数: 4 | 发布时间: 2025-05-17 11:26
文章摘要

Google reversed its decision to revoke the "All files access" permission for the Nextcloud Files app on Android, restoring full functionality after widespread criticism. Initially, Google cited "security concerns" and suggested using alternatives like the Storage Access Framework (SAF) or MediaStore API. However, Nextcloud argued that this was an abuse of power and anti-competitive behavior, similar to recent fines against Apple and Meta by the EU Commission. After Nextcloud publicly criticized Google in a blog post, Google agreed to restore the permission, allowing the app to resume full file syncing capabilities.
