NYT to start searching deleted ChatGPT logs after beating OpenAI in court

作者: miles | 评分: 5 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-07-03 00:23
文章摘要

OpenAI unsuccessfully objected to a court order requiring it to retain ChatGPT logs indefinitely, including deleted and temporary chats, to preserve evidence for a copyright case brought by news organizations like The New York Times. Judge Sidney Stein denied OpenAI's objections, stating the order aligns with legal processes outlined in the user agreement. Although OpenAI plans to continue fighting the order, its options are limited, including the possibility of appealing to the Second Circuit Court of Appeals. Meanwhile, negotiations are ongoing to determine how news plaintiffs can search the logs, with both sides aiming to minimize data retention time. Concerns have been raised about potential privacy breaches during this process, as accessing the logs could expose sensitive user data and impact market dynamics, possibly influencing users to switch to rival AI services.

Huawei releases an open weight model trained on Huawei Ascend GPUs

作者: buyucu | 评分: 305 | 评论数: 18 | 发布时间: 2025-07-02 07:36
文章摘要

本文介绍了一种名为Pangu Pro MoE的混合专家模型（Mixture of Grouped Experts, MoGE），旨在提高大规模语言模型的计算效率。传统MoE模型中，不同专家被激活的频率不均，导致计算负载不平衡。MoGE通过将专家分组并限制每组中被激活的专家数量，实现了更均衡的计算负载分配，尤其在多设备运行时表现优异。Pangu Pro MoE基于Ascend NPUs构建，总参数为720亿，每次激活160亿参数。实验表明，MoGE在训练和推理阶段均提升了效率，推理速度达1148 tokens/s，通过加速可提升至1528 tokens/s，优于同类密集模型。该设计显著改善了计算性能和成本效益。

IntyBASIC: A Basic Compiler for Intellivision

作者: joemanaco | 评分: 6 | 评论数: 1 | 发布时间: 2025-06-29 05:17
文章摘要

这篇文章介绍了Intellivision游戏开发的BASIC编译器——IntyBASIC。作者在开发Intellivision游戏《Space Raid》时，希望简化游戏开发过程，于2013年10月开始开发IntyBASIC，并于2014年1月发布首个版本。IntyBASIC是一个跨平台编译器，可将BASIC源代码转换为CP1610汇编代码，支持Intellivision的视频和声音功能，如SPRITE和SOUND命令。该工具受到了AtariAge社区的欢迎，并促成了多个新游戏和自制游戏的诞生，甚至催生了IntyBASIC编程竞赛。 文章还介绍了IntyBASIC的多个版本更新及其下载途径，建议用户在AtariAge论坛获取最新版本和支持。此外，作者还撰写了两本关于Intellivision游戏编程的书籍，一本针对基础编程，另一本则涵盖更高级技术和实例，如游戏《Oh Mummy!》、《Pumpkin Master》、《Meteor Storm》和《Dungeon Warrior》的源代码和开发技巧。书籍提供了纸质版和电子版购买链接。
