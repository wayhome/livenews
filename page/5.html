It boasts millisecond precision, automatic timezone adjustment via GPS, and maintains accurate time even when powered off through a quartz crystal and watch battery. Key features include a double-buffered display for flicker-free viewing at high framerates, automatic brightness adjustment without PWM, and easy firmware updates. A unique articulated joint allows the clock to switch between a single wide display and a two-line format, requiring multiple processors and displays to stay synchronized. The connection across the hinge uses just four wires for power, data, ground, and display control. The clock's architecture utilizes two processors and six buffer chips to manage the 19-digit display, refreshing it in matrices to minimize overhead and electrical interference. Despite development challenges, including a chip shortage during the pandemic, the final design is robust and user-friendly.
