An ambitious vision of a city built from lava

作者: dabinat | 评分: 16 | 评论数: 2 | 发布时间: 2025-07-07 17:33
文章摘要

Icelandic firm s.ap arkitektar, led by Arnhildur Pálmadóttir and her son Arnar Skarphéðinsson, presented the "Lavaforming" project at the Venice Architecture Biennale. The project proposes using lava from volcanic eruptions as a sustainable building material to create cities, offering an alternative to concrete, which contributes significantly to CO2 emissions. The concept involves directing lava into trenches or using 3D-printing robots to mold it into structural elements like walls and columns. Another method suggests tapping underground magma to cool it into prefabricated architectural pieces. Lava's natural properties, such as its ability to form hard, insulative materials depending on cooling rates, make it a versatile alternative. The project also aims to protect communities from lava flows while utilizing an abundant, renewable resource. Although speculative, the project critiques the environmental impact of conventional construction materials and envisions a futuristic, sustainable approach to architecture.

Zorin OS

作者: oldfuture | 评分: 76 | 评论数: 21 | 发布时间: 2025-07-08 15:37
文章摘要
