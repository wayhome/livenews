Show HN: Coroot – eBPF-based, open source observability with actionable insights

作者: openWrangler | 评分: 127 | 评论数: 11 | 发布时间: 2025-04-08 16:49
文章摘要

Coroot是一个开源的应用性能管理（APM）和可观测性工具，可作为DataDog和NewRelic的替代方案。它通过自动收集指标、日志、 traces 和持续性能分析，提供开箱即用的仪表板和检查功能。Coroot支持零仪器观测，利用eBPF技术自动收集数据，并提供服务地图以覆盖系统所有部分。它还支持SLO（服务级别目标）跟踪、分布式追踪和日志模式识别等功能，帮助用户快速识别和分析异常。Coroot可部署在Docker容器或Kubernetes集群中，并提供成本监控功能，帮助用户理解云支出。此外，Coroot能自动识别超过80%的问题，并通过单个警报发送所有相关检查结果。

文章摘要

文章主要描述了一次由Node镜像补丁更新引发的生产环境多小时停机事件，根本原因与Bash中的通配符扩展及底层文件系统的变化有关。具体来说，Dockerfile中的`CMD`指令使用通配符`/jars/*`来设置Java类路径，但由于未在bash中运行，JVM自行扩展了通配符。在一个POSIX系统中，这依赖于`readdir`系统调用。由于Node镜像更新改变了底层ext4文件系统的目录哈希种子，导致类路径中JAR文件顺序变化，进而引发应用程序初始化失败。 此外，文章还讨论了其他潜在问题，如`buildah`构建镜像时因未缓存层导致类路径优先级问题，以及对`overlayfs`目录遍历顺序的误解，最终通过聚焦底层文件系统问题找到解决方案。

IBM's System/360 (S/360), introduced in 1964, was a transformative mainframe series that revolutionized computing and business operations, despite nearly destroying IBM during its development. The project cost US$5 billion and involved creating a line of 150 products, including 9-track magnetic tape drives. Before the S/360, IBM's popular 1401 mainframe could not be expanded or upgraded, leading to customer demand for compatible and upgradable systems. Internal rivalry between IBM's Endicott and Poughkeepsie engineering teams further complicated matters, as their systems were incompatible. To stay competitive and reduce R&D costs, IBM decided to develop the S/360, aiming for a unified system that could handle various tasks and grow with customer needs. The development process was chaotic, marked by internal conflicts and technological uncertainties, but ultimately succeeded, changing the course of computing history. The S/360's success cemented IBM's leadership and enabled the widespread adoption of computers in the Information Age.
