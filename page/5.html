A Higgs-Bugson in the Linux Kernel

作者: Ne02ptzero | 评分: 192 | 评论数: 17 | 发布时间: 2025-07-02 18:34

本文介绍了作者在Linux内核中遇到的一种难以重现的“Higgs-bugson”问题，影响了处理公司交易数据的系统（Gord）。问题表现为文件复制操作偶尔会失败，出现“权限被拒绝”（-EACCES）的错误，尽管文件权限设置正确。经过调查，作者怀疑问题与NFS和Kerberos认证相关。在NFS使用Kerberos时，内核通过`rpc_gssd`守护进程获取用户凭证，但检查日志未发现凭证问题。为重现问题，作者进行了多种测试，包括生成大文件和创建内存文件系统，最终通过一个内存内虚拟文件系统成功重现并理解了问题根源，涉及Kerberos凭证和NFS交互的复杂性。

文章摘要

Microsoft is phasing out password use in its Authenticator app, starting by disabling the autofill password function in July 2025, followed by the complete removal of saved passwords in August 2025. The company is shifting to passkeys, such as PINs, fingerprints, or facial recognition, as a more secure alternative to traditional passwords. Passkeys use public key cryptography and are stored only on personal devices, reducing risks like phishing and identity theft. Users are advised to transition to passkeys by following Microsoft's prompts in the Authenticator app, which will guide them through setting up a new sign-in method. Passwords can still be stored in Microsoft Edge, but adopting passkeys is recommended for better security.
