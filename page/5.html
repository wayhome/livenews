Brighton and Rottingdean Seashore Electric Railway

作者: 8organicbits | 评分: 3 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-06-29 10:20
文章摘要

The **Brighton and Rottingdean Seashore Electric Railway** was a unique coastal railway in Brighton, England, operating from 1896 to 1901. Designed by Magnus Volk, it extended his existing electric railway by running through shallow coastal waters using a peculiar elevated structure nicknamed **"Daddy Long-Legs"**. The railway, powered by overhead electric lines, faced challenges like storms and tidal impacts. Despite initial popularity, it closed due to financial issues and the construction of sea defenses. The railway's remains were sold for scrap, though some concrete sleepers are still visible at low tide. A model of the railway car is displayed in the Brighton Toy and Model Museum.

本文探讨了詹姆斯·克拉克·麦克斯韦（James Clerk Maxwell）对三色视觉理论（trichromatic theory）的贡献。三色视觉理论解释了人类如何通过三种颜色感知大多数色彩，这一理论常归功于托马斯·杨（Thomas Young）和赫尔曼·冯·亥姆霍兹（Hermann von Helmholtz）。麦克斯韦通过一个复杂的实验装置，利用棱镜和狭缝，展示了如何通过三种特定波长的光（如红、绿、蓝）组合生成不同颜色。他调整狭缝的位置和宽度，控制光的强度和颜色，成功再现了各种色彩。这证明了人眼通过三种颜色可以感知到大多数自然色彩，奠定了现代色彩显示技术的基础。
