Optimizing Common Lisp

作者: todsacerdoti | 评分: 11 | 评论数: 0 | 发布时间: 2025-05-07 09:36
文章摘要

本文介绍了如何使用SBCL内置的统计性能分析工具**sb-sprof**来优化Common Lisp代码，重点分析CPU和内存分配的瓶颈。作者通过分析**parcom/json**模块，成功将运行速度提升了3倍，并将内存分配减少了25倍。文章详细说明了如何使用`with-profiling`宏在CPU模式和内存模式下进行性能分析，识别出如`consume`等热点函数。通过优化技术，如避免不必要的工作、使用`simple-string`和`schar`、多返回值、栈分配和Lambda缓存，有效提升了性能。最终，作者强调了数据驱动优化决策的重要性，而不是仅凭猜测进行优化。

文章摘要

WhatsApp has won a major legal victory against Israeli firm NSO Group, receiving $167 million in punitive damages and $444 million in compensatory damages. NSO was found liable for exploiting a WhatsApp vulnerability to install its Pegasus spyware on roughly 1,400 devices, targeting journalists, activists, and government officials. The "clickless" exploit allowed NSO to hack phones via a simple call. Despite NSO's defense that it only sells to governments for law enforcement, the court ruled against the company. 文章摘要
