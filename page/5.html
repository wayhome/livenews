Jacobi Ellipsoid

作者: perihelions | 评分: 28 | 评论数: 3 | 发布时间: 2025-06-28 16:27
文章摘要

Jacobi ellipsoids are triaxial (scalene) ellipsoids in hydrostatic equilibrium, formed when a uniformly dense, self-gravitating fluid body rotates at a constant angular velocity. Named after Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi, they represent a type of equilibrium shape beyond the previously known Maclaurin spheroids. Unlike Maclaurin spheroids, Jacobi ellipsoids have three unequal axes. The angular velocity and semi-principal axes \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) of a Jacobi ellipsoid are related through complex integrals involving elliptic functions. Unlike the Dedekind ellipsoid, which maintains a fixed orientation with internal fluid flow, the Jacobi ellipsoid rotates as a whole without internal flow.
