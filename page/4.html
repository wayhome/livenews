This decision affected numerous repositories, including the rewrite-java-security project, which had received significant contributions from the open-source community. The author, an early and active contributor, discovered that their work was now under the Moderne Proprietary License (MPL), preventing further public access or contributions without a commercial contract. This shift sets a concerning precedent for open-source stewardship and risks legal and ethical issues.

Weaver Codes: Highly Fault Tolerant Erasure Codes for Storage Systems (2005)

作者: Tomte | 评分: 7 | 评论数: 2 | 发布时间: 2025-05-15 03:38
文章摘要
