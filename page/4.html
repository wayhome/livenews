FDA has approved Yeztugo, a drug that provides protection against HIV infection

文章摘要

SIMD within a register: How I doubled hash table lookup performance

作者: axeluser | 评分: 173 | 评论数: 7 | 发布时间: 2025-07-28 05:41
文章摘要

本文介绍了通过将哈希表中的四个字节桶转换为一个32位整数（uint），作者成功将Cuckoo Filter的查找性能提升了两倍。初始实现使用字节数组表示哈希表，每个桶包含四个槽。作者注意到这四个字节可以视作一个整数，从而通过位操作和移位来加速查找过程。 具体实现中，作者将字节数组替换为uint数组，并通过移位操作检查指纹是否存在于桶中。相比原始的字节数组遍历方式，uint版本的正向查找速度提高了35%，负向查找也有显著提升。作者还尝试了使用BitConverter将uint转换回字节数组，但该方法性能更差。最终，通过简单的整数操作，作者成功优化了哈希表的查找性能。

The Geological Sublime

作者: prismatic | 评分: 56 | 评论数: 3 | 发布时间: 2025-07-28 13:10
文章摘要

Lewis Hyde's essay "The Geological Sublime" explores the intersection of deep geological time, climate change, and human history. He reflects on the awe-inspiring power of earthquakes and other geological events that reveal the immense scales of time, citing Charles Lyell and Darwin's observations of Earth's transformative forces. Hyde contrasts these ancient processes with current rapid climate changes, such as ocean acidification, which are occurring on much shorter timescales. He connects his scientific interests to personal experiences, particularly his enjoyment of observing butterflies, which grounds his understanding of nature's wonders and vulnerabilities in the present day.
