So, 1/3 means one part out of three equal parts. ### Visual Representation Imagine a pizza cut into three equal slices. If you take one slice, you've taken 1/3 of the pizza. ### Decimal and Percentage Conversion - **Decimal Form**: 1 divided by 3 is approximately 0.333... (it's a repeating decimal). - **Percentage**: To convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply by 100. So, 1/3 ≈ 33.33...%. ### Practical Examples 1. **Time**: If you have an hour (60 minutes), 1/3 of an hour is 20 minutes (since 60 ÷ 3 = 20). 2. **Money**: If you have $3, then 1/3 of that is $1. ### Common Misconceptions - **Not Equal to 0.3**: Some might think 1/3 is exactly 0.3, but it's actually 0.333..., which is slightly more than 0.3. - **Not Equal to 33%**: While 1/3 is approximately 33.33%, it's not exactly 33%. ### Comparing with Other Fractions - **1/2**: 1/2 is larger than 1/3 (since 0.5 > 0.333...). - **1/4**: 1/4 is smaller than 1/3 (since 0.25 < 0.333...). ### Applications in Real Life - **Recipes**: If a recipe calls for 1/3 cup of sugar, you're being asked for one part out of three equal parts of a cup. - **Discounts**: If a store offers a 1/3 discount, it means you pay 2/3 of the original price. ### Final Answer 1/3 represents one part out of three equal parts. It's a fundamental fraction that's used in various real-life scenarios, from dividing time to splitting costs. It's approximately equal to 0.333... in decimal form and 33.33...% in percentage form.
