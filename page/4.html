文章摘要

BYD begins testing solid-state EV batteries in the Seal

作者: toomuchtodo | 评分: 104 | 评论数: 11 | 发布时间: 2025-06-20 20:42
文章摘要

BYD has begun testing solid-state batteries in its Seal electric sedan, with initial tests showing a potential driving range of nearly 1,200 miles (1,875 km). These batteries, with an energy density of 400 Wh/kg, nearly double that of current lithium-ion batteries, can gain 932 miles (1,500 km) of range in just 12 minutes of charging. BYD aims to start installing these batteries in production vehicles by 2027, with limited production until 2029, followed by mass production in 2030. The Seal, competing with Tesla's Model 3, will be the first model to feature these batteries. BYD also plans to reduce costs, aiming for solid-state batteries to match the price of liquid lithium-ion batteries by the end of the decade.
