What Is "Induced Atmospheric Vibration"?

作者: belter | 评分: 117 | 评论数: 16 | 发布时间: 2025-04-29 12:30
文章摘要

"Induced Atmospheric Vibration" refers to a phenomenon where extreme temperature variations cause anomalous oscillations in very high voltage power lines (400 kV), leading to synchronization failures in electrical systems. This effect, observed during a blackout on the Iberian Peninsula, results from atmospheric conditions like temperature and humidity affecting corona discharges around power lines. These discharges, influenced by environmental factors, introduce fluctuations in the electrical grid's reactance, complicating voltage and frequency control. The oscillations can lead to cascading failures in interconnected power systems, as seen in the widespread disturbances across Europe.
