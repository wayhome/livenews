LVGL（Light and Versatile Graphics Library）是一个成熟、开源的嵌入式图形库，用于为各种微控制器（MCU）、微处理器（MPU）和显示类型创建美观的用户界面（UI）。它获得了Arm、STM32、NXP、Arduino等知名厂商的支持，具有丰富的功能，包括30+内置控件、强大的样式系统、类似Flexbox和Grid的布局引擎，以及支持多语言的排版系统。LVGL仅需32kB RAM和128kB Flash，兼容多种显示设备，并可在多种操作系统下运行。它基于MIT许可，适用于商业项目，提供MicroPython和PikaScript绑定，支持Make和CMake构建系统，并允许在PC上开发并移植到嵌入式设备。LVGL团队还提供UI设计和咨询服务，用户可通过GitHub Sponsors进行捐赠以支持其发展。

Breaking up with vibe coding

作者: isfttr | 评分: 49 | 评论数: 16 | 发布时间: 2025-03-30 00:17
文章摘要

"Vibe coding" refers to a coding approach where developers, aided by AI agents, immerse themselves in the flow of coding without a strict plan, relying on intuition and inspiration. While it feels productive, this method can lead to issues. The author experienced frustration with vibe coding due to time inefficiency, high costs from extensive AI usage, and the need for frequent rework when lacking a clear structure. Although AI helps in exploring initial ideas and generating code quickly, over-reliance can cause misunderstandings and "AI hallucinations"—producing plausible but incorrect solutions. The author contrasts vibe coding with AI chat and web search, noting that each has its strengths and weaknesses. AI chat offers quick answers but may lead to dependence without deep understanding. Web search is valuable for specific solutions but can be time-consuming. To balance productivity and cost, the author recommends tools like Gemini Code Assist for creating unit tests and suggests avoiding expensive AI agents like Roo Code or Cline.
