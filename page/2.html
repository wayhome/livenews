Yes, there is an SAO connector standard for SAO (Shitty/Silly Add-Ons)! SAO is a connector specification originally created for electronic conference badges. The standard defines: - **Connector**: 2x3 pin header (6 pins total) - **Pinout**: - 2x GPIO pins - 2x power pins (typically 3.3V) - 2x ground pins - **Physical**: Standard 2.54mm (0.1") pitch headers The connector allows small PCB add-ons to be attached to a host badge or device. It's intentionally simple to encourage creative, fun, and sometimes purposefully over-the-top or silly expansions. There are various SAO designs available in the maker/hacker community, ranging from simple LED displays to interactive sensors and games. The standard has become popular at conferences like DEF CON, Supercon, and other hardware hacking events.
